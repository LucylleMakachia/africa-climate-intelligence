# Interview Guide

## Research purpose

Understand how organisations and practitioners in Kenya
access, assess, integrate and use weather and climate
information, and identify barriers and unmet needs.

## Interview duration

20–30 minutes

## Opening

Thank you for taking the time to speak with me.

I am researching how organisations currently find, access,
process and use weather and climate information, particularly
for humanitarian, development, research and operational work.

The purpose of this interview is to understand your experience
and current workflow. I am not trying to sell or validate a
specific product, so I am interested in both what works well
and what could be improved.

## A. Background

1. Could you briefly describe your role?
2. What type of work does your organisation do?
3. How does weather or climate information feature in your work?

## B. Recent use

4. Think about the most recent time you needed weather or
   climate information. What were you trying to accomplish?

5. Where did you obtain the information?

6. How did you find or choose that source?

7. What did you do with the information after obtaining it?

## C. Data and tools

8. What platforms, datasets or tools do you normally use?

9. Do you combine climate/weather information with other
   datasets?

10. What tools do you use for that process?

## D. Friction

11. Which part of the process is most difficult or time-consuming?

12. What do you normally do when you encounter that problem?

13. How often does this happen?

14. Who else becomes involved?

15. What are the consequences when the information is difficult
    to obtain or use?

## E. Existing solutions

16. What currently works well?

17. What platforms or services have been particularly useful?

18. What do you still have to do manually?

19. Are there capabilities you wish existing platforms provided?

## F. Improvement

20. If you could change one thing about the current process,
    what would it be?

21. Is there anything important about your workflow that I
    haven't asked about?

## Closing

Thank the participant and ask whether they are comfortable
being contacted for clarification or follow-up.
