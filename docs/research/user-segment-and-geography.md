# Primary User Segment and Initial Geography

## Day 5 Decision

### Primary User Segment

**Humanitarian and development organizations working in Kenya.**

The initial users within this segment will primarily be:

* Information management teams
* GIS/spatial teams
* Monitoring and evaluation teams
* Programme teams
* Climate-resilience teams
* Disaster-risk and preparedness teams

The platform will initially focus on organizational users who need to obtain, combine, monitor, visualize, interpret, or report weather and climate information for specific projects and geographic areas.

---

## Initial Geography

**Kenya**

Kenya will be the initial geographic focus for research, product development, and user testing.

The platform should be designed with an architecture that can eventually support expansion to other African countries, but geographic expansion will not be part of the initial MVP.

---

## Why This User Segment?

The selection is based on the initial desk research and platform analysis conducted during Days 3 and 4.

Humanitarian and development organizations may require multiple types of information for programme planning, monitoring, preparedness, risk assessment and reporting.

Potentially relevant information includes:

* Weather observations
* Climate indicators
* Forecasts
* Drought information
* Flood information
* Hazard information
* Population data
* Administrative boundaries
* Infrastructure
* Project locations
* Agriculture and livelihood information

This creates a potential need for information integration and decision-oriented workflows rather than simply another source of raw climate data.

The hypothesis requires validation through user research.

---

## Why Kenya?

Kenya has an established climate-information ecosystem involving institutions and platforms including:

* Kenya Meteorological Department
* ICPAC
* NDMA
* NEMA
* Other government, research, humanitarian and development organizations

This makes Kenya an appropriate initial research environment because the project can investigate how existing information is accessed and used rather than assuming that climate information is absent.

The initial research question is therefore concerned with the relationship between:

**information availability → access → integration → interpretation → practical use.**

---

## Why Not Expand to Africa Immediately?

A Kenya-first approach allows the project to:

1. Understand one specific information ecosystem.
2. Conduct manageable user research.
3. Identify locally relevant workflows.
4. Test data sources and licensing.
5. Build and evaluate an MVP.
6. Establish evidence of user value.
7. Develop a reusable technical architecture.
8. Consider expansion to other African countries after validation.

Africa-wide expansion remains a longer-term objective rather than an initial product requirement.

---

## Initial Product Hypothesis

> Humanitarian and development organizations in Kenya may benefit from a system that brings together relevant weather, climate, hazard, and geospatial information and transforms it into accessible, location-specific intelligence for planning, monitoring, analysis and reporting.

This is a **product hypothesis**, not a validated conclusion.

It must be tested through interviews, workflow observation, prototype testing, and further evidence collection.

---

## Secondary User Groups

The following groups remain part of the broader opportunity but will not be the primary design target for the initial MVP:

* County governments
* Agricultural organizations
* GIS/environmental consultants
* Researchers and universities
* Infrastructure organizations
* Financial/insurance organizations
* Water-resource organizations
* Climate-sensitive businesses
* Individual farmers

Their needs may be investigated where they overlap with the primary user segment or where research indicates a significant opportunity.

---

## Decision Status

**Primary user:** Humanitarian and development organizations

**Initial geography:** Kenya

**MVP geography:** To be determined through further research

**Confidence:** Preliminary

**Validation required:** Yes

**Next validation activity:** Stakeholder/user interviews and workflow research
