# Kenya Climate Information Evidence Matrix

## Purpose

This document records evidence about climate and weather information availability, accessibility, usability, integration, interpretation, monitoring, and decision-making in Kenya.

The purpose is to identify recurring problems and opportunities that could inform the design of the Africa Climate Intelligence platform.

Findings should be supported by identifiable sources wherever possible.

## Evidence Strength

### High

Use for:

* Peer-reviewed research
* Government publications
* Official datasets
* Direct interviews
* Formal evaluations
* Strong institutional evidence

### Medium

Use for:

* NGO reports
* Development-agency reports
* Institutional research
* Conference publications

### Low / Exploratory

Use for:

* News articles
* Blogs
* Practitioner discussions
* Social media
* Unsourced or weakly sourced claims

Low-strength evidence can help identify questions for further investigation but should not normally be treated as proof.
