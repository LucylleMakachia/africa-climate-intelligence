# Climate & Weather Platform Analysis

## Purpose

This document analyzes existing climate and weather information platforms to understand:

* What they provide
* Who they serve
* How users access information
* What technologies/features they provide
* What limitations users may encounter
* Where potential opportunities may exist

The analysis should remain descriptive and evidence-based. Potential gaps should be treated as hypotheses until supported by user research or other evidence.
